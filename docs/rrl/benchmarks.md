### 1. GCash

**Developer**: Mynt Inc.

**Features Present**:

- **Account & Security**  
  - One account per mobile number  
  - PIN and biometric (fingerprint) login  
  - OTPs (One-Time Passwords) for authentication
  - Transaction history with reference numbers, amounts, dates, and counterparty details  
  - GScore (user credit/behaviour score)  

- **Wallet Management**  
  - View wallet balance per account  
  - Cash in (add balance): online via partner banks and Instapay, and over‑the‑counter (Cebuana Lhuillier, Villarica, Touchpay, etc.)  
  - Request money via QR code generation  
  - Cash out (withdraw) via partner outlets  

- **Send & Transfer**  
  - Real‑time GCash‑to‑GCash transfers (free)  
  - Send to multiple GCash accounts at once (Send to Many)  
  - Express Send (quick transfer to a GCash account)  
  - GCash Padala (send to anyone in the country)  
  - Bank transfers: local (BPI, BDO, UnionBank, Landbank, Metrobank, Chinabank, etc.) and international  
  - Save frequently used bank account details for faster future transfers  

- **Payments**  
  - Scan to Pay: GCash QR, QRPh (70,000+ merchants nationwide), and Alipay+ QR (select international merchants)  
  - GCash Card: contactless tap‑to‑pay (NFC‑enabled phones) and use in 200+ countries/territories  
  - Online payments: secure checkout on e‑commerce platforms, with up to ₱20k scam protection (Online Shopping Protect)  
  - Pay directly for App Store, Play Store, and subscription services  
  - Bills payment: 400+ billers covering electric utilities, water utilities, cable/internet, telecoms, credit cards, loans, government agencies, insurance, transportation, real estate, healthcare, schools, payment solutions, and charities/foundations  
  - Set payment reminders and save recurring biller profiles  

- **Mobile Load & Subscriptions**  
  - Buy load for all mobile networks  
  - Exclusive Globe/TM promos  
  - Prepaid broadband, TV channel packages, prepaid health insurance, and eSIM load  
  - Borrow Load (advance load of ₱50–₱299, payable up to 14 days)  

- **Lending & Credit**  
  - GCredit: up to ₱50k credit limit, 0.17% daily interest, 30‑day billing cycle (interest ranges 0–6.99% monthly; APR 22.1–89.9%; processing fees 0–7.5%)  
  - GLoan: up to ₱150k, 1.59% monthly interest, instalment terms up to 24 months  
  - GGives: up to ₱125k, zero downpayment, instalments from 3 to 24 months  
  - Borrow from Fuse and CIMB (GCredit)  

- **Wealth Management (Save & Invest)**  
  - GSave: marketplace for savings accounts with partner banks; high interest rates, no minimum deposit or balance, easy account opening  
  - GStocks: trade local stocks with instant approval, minimum ₱500  
  - GFunds: professionally managed funds, risk profile assessment, minimum ₱50  
  - GBonds: fixed‑income investments  
  - GCrypto: curated cryptocurrencies with instant approval, minimum ₱200  
  - Learning Hub for investment education  

- **Insurance**  
  - Hospitalisation & accident insurance: starting at ₱59, up to ₱1.2M coverage for medical expenses and critical illnesses  
  - Travel insurance: as fast as 5 minutes, starting at ₱350, up to ₱2.5M coverage  
  - Scam Protection for express send transactions: ₱30 for 30 days, up to ₱15k coverage  

- **Lifestyle & Rewards**  
  - GForest: earn energy points through transactions, partners plant real trees in the Philippines  
  - GDeals / Food Hub / Near Deals: location‑based discounts and food offers  
  - A+ Rewards and raffle promotions (e.g., Win 500k!)  
  - Shop, Wellness, Earn Money, Jobs, Ship & Deliver, Play, Travel, Commute, Gigs, Tax Refund, and GBayanihan within the app ecosystem  

- **Profile & Settings**  
  - Account information: mobile number, verification status (government ID required), birth date, nationality, email, current address  
  - Profile Limits, Subscription Center, My Linked Accounts, My QR Codes  
  - Privacy choices, Terms & Conditions, Alipay+ integration, Voucher Pocket, Promos  
  - Help centre and Log Out

### 3. Lemoneyd - Finance Tracker

**Developer**: Lemoneyd

**Features Present**:

- **Account & Savings Tracking**  
  - Monitor all savings accounts in one place  
  - Set savings goals and track progress  
  - Total balance overview with per-bank breakdown  
  - View all accounts with drill-down capability

- **Expense Tracking**  
  - Categorise transactions for spending monitoring  
  - Set budgets per category  
  - Log and manage all transactions with date and time stamps  
  - Add new transactions via in-app button

- **Transaction Log**  
  - Centralised transaction list with timestamps  
  - Add button for manual entry

- **Insights & Analytics (Finsights)**  
  - Deposit overview with per-month viewing  
  - Total deposits per period  
  - Source of deposits breakdown with individual deposit amounts  
  - Bar graph visualising annual deposits  
  - Expense analysis (basic breakdown)

- **Time Deposit Management**  
  - Add and track time deposit accounts  
  - List of banks/institutions with interest rates per annum (p.a.)

- **Institution Coverage**  
  - Supports local digital banks, e-wallets, and traditional banks commonly used in the Philippines (per user feedback)

- **Monetisation & User Experience**  
  - Freemium model: free version available; subscription-based Pro plan  
  - Subscription fee noted as affordable per user feedback  
  - Requires online/network access (no offline mode; per user feedback)  
  - Account sign-up required (no Gmail OAuth shortcut; per user feedback)

    - connect accounts and manage all things finance in one comprehensive dashboard
    - take control of your money with custom budgets
    - keep track of your monthly bills and subscriptions
    - monitor your cash flow and balance trend
    - track stocks alongside your other accounts
    - manage savings to plan for your future
    - get insightful reports and financial tips
    - customize your dashboard to see what matters most to you
    - avoid overspending with predictive alerts
    - share accounts and track together with family and friends
    - sync your data across all your devices with secure cloud backup
- your finances in one place
    - Wallet is the money manager and bill tracker designed to help you from day one. Utilize in depth reports and statistics on all your spending, accounts and investments to always know where you stand. Your financial data is securely synced to the cloud, so you can access it from any device and gain the continuous access you need to stay in control of your finances. Whether you need to save money until payday or budget for the long-term, Wallet is flexible for your changing needs. Track your stock portfolio in Wallet and combine your stock holdings with other assets and grow your wealth by building a diversified portfolio of stocks, ETFs and other funds.
- Take control of your finances with Wallet 
🔗Automatic Bank Updates - Track where every dollar is going by seamlessly connecting your accounts. Transactions are automatically and securely synced, then smartly categorized, and factored into your budget.

🚀 Seamless Setup & Data Import - Get a complete picture of your finances from day one. Wallet utilizes a temporary foreground service during your initial bank connection or profile sync to securely download up to 3 months of historical transactions.

💰Flexible Budgets - Whatever it is you need to accomplish, from paying off debt to buying a car or saving for retirement, this budgeting app offers the flexibility to meet your goals and cleverly react to any changing financial circumstances. With Wallet, budgeting expenses has never been easier.

⏰Planned Payments - Never miss a due date with this bill tracker. Organize bills and subscriptions and keep track of due dates. See upcoming payments and how the payments will impact your cash flow.

🤝Sharing selected accounts - Selected accounts can be shared with your spouse, family, friends or colleagues who need to cooperate on a budget. Whether you’re buying your first home with your partner or managing household costs with roommates, everyone can contribute from any platform. Track your spending together!

📊Insightful reports - Wallet's financial overviews give you actionable insights about the state of your finances, across accounts, cards, debts and cash. Gain insights on where you should be budgeting more or could save more money.

🗂Imports or Manual Updates - You can now import all your transaction data from sources of your choice so you will get a full report for simple expense tracking. Be it from your bank or your own spreadsheets.

💱Multicurrency - Multicurrency accounts and worldwide bank coverage makes Wallet the perfect partner for expats, international students and travelers.

Technical Note : To provide a seamless experience, Wallet uses foreground services to perform data transfers during initial bank synchronization and profile setup. This ensures that your data is imported securely and completely, even if you navigate away from the app during the process.
