problem_and_motivation: Filipino cultural values like utang na loob are often misunderstood and misused, leading to unhealthy social pressures and a perception of toxicity. This distortion threatens the preservation of authentic Filipino identity and social realities, particularly among younger generations exposed to globalization and Western frameworks.
approach:
  - A qualitative survey design was used to explore personal definitions and contextual perceptions of utang na loob.
  - Focus group discussions were conducted with 13 college students aged 18-21 to gather rich, narrative data.
  - In Vivo coding was employed to capture intrinsic meanings from participants' statements, prioritizing their perspectives.
  - Thematic analysis categorized responses into themes reflecting positive attributes and critical perspectives on the value.
  - English translations of participant statements were reviewed by a linguist to ensure accuracy for a broader audience.
findings:
  - num: 13 college students participated, providing narratives that revealed both positive and negative perceptions of utang na loob.
  - Utang na loob is perceived as rooted in kindness and essential for maintaining good social ties and giving back.
  - The value is also viewed as a form of obligation and, by some, as a negative Filipino trait that can be a trap.
  - Family contexts show utang na loob as honoring parental sacrifices but also as a source of disputes over inheritance.
  - In collegial relationships, the value fosters mutual support but can create ongoing indebtedness beyond monetary repayment.
  - In politics, utang na loob is exploited through the Padrino system for cronyism and political ambition.
  - The study highlights the gap between the ideal of utang na loob as goodwill and its practice as coercive obligation.
  - Misuse of the value leads to emotional and financial pressure on recipients, making them feel helpless.
  - The continuity of indebtedness, even after repayment, is identified as a problematic aspect of this cultural value.
  - Participants' varied definitions suggest an awareness of the core topic, likely influenced by their academic background.
