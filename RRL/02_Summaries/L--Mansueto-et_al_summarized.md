tldr: Retirees in Siquijor prioritize essential utilities, groceries, and healthcare, while spending minimally on housing, insurance, and leisure due to cultural support systems and financial constraints.
problem_and_motivation: Financial literacy gaps and rising healthcare costs leave many retirees, particularly in the Philippines, financially vulnerable. Understanding the specific spending habits and cultural factors of retirees in Siquijor is necessary to design targeted policy interventions. Such research is missing for this specific demographic context.
approach:
  - The study employed a descriptive quantitative research design.
  - A purposive sample of 179 retirees who had permanently left the workforce was selected.
  - Data was collected using a structured survey questionnaire with a Likert scale.
  - Weighted mean calculations and composite mean analysis were used to assess spending frequency.
  - The questionnaire underwent expert validation and pilot testing to ensure clarity and reliability.
