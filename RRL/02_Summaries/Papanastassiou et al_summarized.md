  - "Method: Deep Q-Network with LSTM encoder to process sliding windows of transaction sequences as states."
  - "Reward shaping: asymmetric rewards (r1=4 for fraud detection, r2=1 for normal) to incentivize rare event capture."

  - "Orthogonality analysis: dynamic overlap of detected frauds across thresholds to assess complementarity."
  - "UCI benchmark: transformed to long format to create 6-step sequences for sequential model."
