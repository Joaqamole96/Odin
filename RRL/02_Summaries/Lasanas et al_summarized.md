  - num: The study identified three main themes: personal, positional, and ideological narratives.

  - They are positioned as second parents, primary financial providers, and active decision-makers in the household.
  - Panganays internalize familial obligations, view their role as a source of empowerment and hope.
  - Despite challenges, panganays find fulfillment in giving back out of love and will.
