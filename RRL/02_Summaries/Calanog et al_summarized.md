problem_and_motivation: Research on Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) features has often focused on general positive or negative impacts, leaving a gap in understanding the holistic user experience. This paper addresses that gap by exploring the specific motivations, benefits, and challenges faced by users of Shopee's Spay Later feature. Understanding these user experiences is crucial for informing the responsible design and use of such financial tools.
approach:
  - A qualitative phenomenological study approach was used to explore the lived experiences of Spay Later users.
  - Data was collected through semi-structured interviews with ten participants selected via judgmental sampling.
  - Participants were required to have at least a 5,000 PHP credit limit and a Gold or Platinum Shopee membership tier.
  - Data analysis was conducted using thematic analysis to identify patterns and themes in participant responses.
  - All interviews were conducted online or face-to-face with strict adherence to ethical protocols.
findings:
  - num: 10 participants were interviewed, all loyal Shopee customers with Gold or Platinum status and a 5,000 PHP minimum credit limit.
  - Spay Later users exhibited three primary usage patterns: occasional use for necessities, regular monthly use for essentials, and needs-based use during financial constraints.
  - The main factors for using Spay Later were limited budgets, the ability to afford high-cost items over time, attractive incentives, and urgent needs.
  - Key challenges included transaction errors, unexpected fees, insufficient credit balance, and concerns over seller accountability.
  - Users reported that Spay Later positively influenced their financial habits, enhancing budgeting skills and encouraging self-reflection.
