approach:
  - Quantitative-correlational design surveyed 115 active investors in Metro Manila.

  - Applied Chi-square Test for Independence and Cramer's V to assess associations between demographic profiles, social factors, and investment behaviors.
  - Analyzed data using Jamovi statistical software, focusing on relationships between social influences, self-assessed risk tolerance, and investment types.

  - Social influences showed no significant association with the specific types of investments chosen by respondents.
  - Demographic factors like sex, age, and educational background showed no statistically significant influence on peer pressure or social norms.
