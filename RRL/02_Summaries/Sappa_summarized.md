tldr: "An AI dual-engine system combining reinforcement learning for portfolio optimization and XGBoost for risk profiling outperforms traditional methods in Sharpe ratio and classification accuracy."
problem_and_motivation: "Traditional risk profiling relies on static questionnaires and mean-variance optimization, which fail to capture dynamic investor behavior and non-linear market responses. This gap leads to misaligned portfolios and poor risk-adjusted returns. An adaptive, AI-driven approach is needed for personalized and responsive FinTech investment platforms."
approach:
  - "Data from 20,000 simulated FinTech users over 36 months, including transaction logs, demographics, and behavioral proxies."
  - "Reinforcement learning agent (DQN variant) optimizes portfolio allocation with state features including weights, returns, and volatility."
  - "XGBoost classifier and K-Means clustering (5 clusters) for dynamic user risk profiling and segmentation."
  - "Training in synthetic market environments with 10,000 episodes and event-driven rebalancing triggered by market shocks or behavioral deviations."
  - "Benchmarked against Mean-Variance Optimization, rule-based allocation, and randomized filtering baselines."
  - "Modular microservices deployment with Docker, Kubernetes, and REST APIs for real-time inference."
findings:
  - "num: Reinforcement learning agent achieved a Sharpe ratio of 1.45, outperforming Mean-Variance Optimization (0.92), rule-based (1.05), and XGBoost static (1.28) models."
  - "num: XGBoost classifier achieved an F1 score of 0.86, AUC of 0.91, recall of 0.89, and precision of 0.84 for risk classification."
  - "AI models produced lower Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) values than classical methods, indicating superior portfolio diversification."
  - "Risk profiles evolved dynamically, with younger investors (25-40) showing higher risk tolerance and returns (7.5%-8.1%) versus older investors (55+) with conservative returns (3.8%-4.4%)."
  - "Feature importance analysis identified income stability, investment horizon, and drawdown tolerance as key predictors of risk profile."
  - "The hybrid clustering and classification approach enabled continuous monitoring and recalibration of user risk profiles."
