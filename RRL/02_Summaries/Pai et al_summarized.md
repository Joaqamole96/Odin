tldr: "Proposes OldTransfer and NewTransfer metrics and synthetic drifting datasets, and finds lifelong learning preserves old knowledge while domain adaptation adapts to new covariate drift."
problem_and_motivation: "Existing concept drift research lacks clear-defined incremental drift datasets and proper evaluation protocols. Most methods assume instance-wise data streams, ignoring batch-wise practical scenarios. Metrics like backward and forward transfer do not accurately reflect knowledge transfer under incremental drift."
approach:
  - "Proposes OldTransfer and NewTransfer to measure knowledge preservation and adaptation across sequential batches."
  - "Generates synthetic incremental drift datasets with covariate, actual, and concept drift types using feature-based and GAN-based methods."
  - "Evaluates domain adaptation methods (DANN, MCD, GST, ARM) and lifelong learning methods (EWC, GEM) on generated and real datasets."
  - "Uses convolutional networks for images and MLP for tabular data, with standard validation splits per batch."
  - "Compares against finetune, joint training, and joint-full as baselines."

  - "num: On Forest Cover dataset, GEM achieves OldTransfer of 0.1785, outperforming finetune baseline (0.0) and joint training (0.1586)."
