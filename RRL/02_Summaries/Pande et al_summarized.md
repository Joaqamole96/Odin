approach:
  - A systematic literature review was conducted using the PRISMA framework to ensure a structured and transparent search process.
  - The Scopus database was searched using keywords related to personal financial planning and behavioral finance.
  - The search was limited to documents published between 2004 and 2024, focusing on English-language articles, conference papers, and reviews.
  - Documents were filtered by subject area (e.g., Economics, Business), document type, and publication stage, resulting in a final sample of 158 documents.
  - A content analysis was performed on the final set to identify trends in publications, prominent journals, contributing institutions, and geographical distribution.
