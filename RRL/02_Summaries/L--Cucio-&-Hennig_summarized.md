problem_and_motivation: "The rapid advancement of AI and gen-AI tools may significantly transform labor markets globally, but its specific impact on the Philippines—particularly its large BPO sector—remains underexplored. There is a need to map occupational exposure and complementarity to inform policy and mitigate potential job displacement."
approach:
  - "Merged AI exposure scores from Felten et al. (2021) and complementarity scores from Pizzinelli et al. (2023) with October 2022 Philippine Labor Force Survey microdata (183,602 observations)."
  - "Used 4-digit ISCO-08 occupation codes for granular analysis, linking them to U.S. O*NET task data via a crosswalk."
  - "Categorized occupations into high exposure/high complementarity, high exposure/low complementarity, and low exposure groups based on median scores."
  - "Analyzed correlations with demographic indicators including gender, age, education, wage, location, and sector."
  - "Compared findings with peer Asian economies using ILO aggregated data, noting caveats regarding granularity."
